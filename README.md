An entry for the make.fun bounty "Worst Superpower": invent the most
useless superpower in one line of at most 140 characters — a power so
pointless that naming it is the whole joke.

## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```text
I can teleport anywhere on Earth — but only to the exact spot where I'm already standing.
```

89 characters, one line.

## Why this is the worst superpower

Teleportation's entire point is going somewhere else — instantly,
anywhere, no travel. This version keeps the flash, the vanishing, the
dramatic reappearance… and cancels the point completely: the hero
reappears in the exact spot they just left, out of breath, having gone
nowhere.

It is a real superpower with a real name, stated in one breath
alongside exactly why it achieves nothing. The constraint is not a
side effect or a curse — it is the power itself, used as intended,
accomplishing zero. And it is easy to picture in use: a villain
monologues, the hero shouts "nowhere is safe from me!", vanishes in a
burst of light — and is still standing right there, in the villain's
way, having spent the whole trick.

It is not one of the familiar gags: no slow flight, no invisibility
while nobody watches, no talking to animals. The specific detail —
"the exact spot where I'm already standing" — fixes the power in the
world instead of leaving it vague.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
